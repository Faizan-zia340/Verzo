## Changi Lahori social post kiven hondi ay

- **Pehli line rok lendi ay.** Sawal, mazahiya jumla, ya koi gall jo parhan wala apne naal jor sake — pehli line hi sab kuch ay.
- **Lehja apna honda ay.** Lahori gallan vich "yar", "ki haal ay", "mazaa aa gaya" jehe lafz natural lagde ne. Penlix nu tone friendly rakho te post apne aap Lahori rang pakar laindi ay.
- **Chhoti te saaf hondi ay.** Lambi post koi nahi parhda. Do-teen chhote jumle, aik wadhiya line — bas.
- **Aakhir vich gall karan da moka honda ay.** "Tusi ki kehnde o?" ya "Comment vich daso" — jehri gall reader nu jawab den layi majboor kare.
- **Emoji ik do hi kaafi ne.** Ik do emoji post nu chamka dinde ne, par zyada emoji naal post halki lagdi ay.

## 5 tips — Lahori style layi

- **Audience daso.** "Lahore de students layi" ya "food lovers layi" — audience likhan naal post di zubaan te misalan badal jandiyan ne.
- **Lahore diyan misalan mango.** "Har point naal Lahore di aik misaal deyo" likho — nihari, paye, lassi, ya Liberty di sair — post vich jaan aa jandi ay.
- **Length short rakho.** Social post chhota hi changa lagda ay. Length short te rakho.
- **Voice input vi use kar sakde o.** Je type karna slow lagda ay, Speak button dabao te bol ke prompt deyo. Chrome te Edge vich chalda ay; tuhadi awaaz record nahi hondi te kise server te nahi jandi.
- **Generate to baad aik vaari parh lo.** Naam, jagah te number theek ne ya nahi — post karan to pehlan check kar lo.
