## You have the photo. Penlix writes the words.

Some blog posts start with a blank page. Others start with a photo — a product shot, a place you visited, a chart, a meal, a sunset. You know the photo is worth a post, but turning it into words takes an hour you do not have.

That is what image-to-blog is for. Upload a photo to [Penlix](/register), pick Blog, and Penlix writes a full blog post about what is in the picture. You do not even need to type a prompt.

## How it works — 3 steps

**Step 1: Upload the photo.** On the Generator page, use the image upload control and pick a photo from your device. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB, one image per generation. The photo is used for one generation and then discarded — it is never stored.

**Step 2: Pick Blog.** Select Blog as the content type. That is all the setup there is.

**Step 3: Generate — prompt optional.** Click Generate. You can leave the prompt completely empty and Penlix will still write about the photo. If you want a specific angle — "focus on the colours" or "write it for beginners" — type that as the prompt and Penlix follows it.

That is the whole flow. No topic field to fill, no audience to pick, no prompt to write — unless you want to.

## A real test: a mountain photo, no prompt

We tested this live on 26 November 2026. A simple mountain drawing — green field, two brown mountains, a yellow sun — was uploaded with Blog selected and the prompt left empty. Penlix produced a full blog post about the picture, starting:

> This image presents a simple, stylized landscape. At its base, a vibrant green field stretches across the frame, suggesting fertile ground under an open sky. Rising from this field are two prominent brown mountains, depicted with clean, geometric lines.

The post went on to describe the blue sky, the yellow sun in the top right and the flat-colour composition, and ended with a closing thought: "Consider how simple elements can create a powerful impression." It also came with an SEO package (meta title "Minimalist Landscape Art: Mountains, Sun, Green Field") and a Reality Check score of 9 out of 10 for Human-ness, 8 for Punchiness and 10 for No-Cliché.

The Reality Check is Penlix scoring its own draft out of 10 — it is self-scoring, not a second reviewer — so treat a low score as a hint to regenerate or tune, not as a guarantee.

The generation cost 1 credit. Free signup comes with 20 free credits.

## What makes a good photo for image-to-blog

- **One clear subject.** A single dish, one product, one view. Penlix describes what it can see — a busy photo with ten things in it gets a scattered post.
- **Good light, sharp focus.** If you cannot tell what is in the photo, neither can the AI. Blurry or very dark photos produce vague posts.
- **A photo with a story.** A finished cake, a before-and-after, a place at golden hour — photos that already suggest a story get the best posts.
- **Your own photo.** Use photos you own or have the right to use. Penlix writes about the picture; the rights to the picture are yours to sort out.

## When to add a prompt (and when not to)

**Leave it empty when:** you want a straight description of the photo, or you are not sure what angle to take. The empty-prompt result is a good first draft to react to.

**Add a prompt when:** you want a specific angle, audience or tone. Three useful prompts:

- "Write this for first-time visitors — keep it simple and welcoming."
- "Focus on the details and colours, like an art review."
- "Turn this into a short how-to: what is in the photo and how to do it yourself."

A prompt of one sentence is enough. The photo does most of the work.

## What you get with the post

Every image-to-blog generation returns the same package as a text blog:

- **The blog post itself** — opening hook, organised body, conclusion.
- **An SEO package** — meta title, meta description and 5 keywords, so the post is ready to publish.
- **A Reality Check score** — Human-ness, Punchiness and No-Cliché out of 10.
- **A font picker** — change the font of the generated post on the output card (Default, Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lora, Merriweather), and copy or export it as TXT, PDF or DOCX.

## Common mistakes

1. **Uploading a screenshot of text.** Penlix writes about what it sees, not a transcription. A photo of a paragraph becomes a post *about* a photo of a paragraph.
2. **Expecting it to know the context.** The AI sees the photo, not your memories of taking it. If the place or the backstory matters, add one sentence as a prompt.
3. **Publishing without reading.** The post describes the photo — check that it got the details right before you publish. A wrong colour or a miscounted object is easy to miss and easy to fix.
4. **Using a stock photo you do not own.** The writing is yours; the image rights are a separate question.

## FAQ

**Do I need to write a prompt?**
No. Upload the photo, pick Blog and press Generate. A prompt is only for steering the angle, audience or tone.

**What image formats work?**
JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB, one image per generation.

**How much does it cost?**
1 credit per generation. Free signup includes 20 credits; [pricing](/pricing) starts at $4.99 for 50 credits.

**Is my photo stored anywhere?**
No. The image is held in memory for the one generation and then discarded — nothing is saved to the database.

**Can I use image-to-blog for products?**
Yes — product photos are one of the best uses. Upload the product shot, add a prompt like "write a product story for new customers", and you get a post you can publish on your store blog.
