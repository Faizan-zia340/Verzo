## Urdu mein content ki kami

Pakistan mein Urdu bolne walon ki tadad karoron mein hai, lekin internet par Urdu mein quality content bohot kam hai. Zyada tar blogs, guides aur articles English mein hain — jab ke aik bari audience aisi hai jo Urdu mein parhna chahti hai. Isi liye Urdu content generator ki talaash barh rahi hai.

Lekin Urdu mein AI content likhne se pehle aik farq samajhna zaroori hai: **Urdu script** (اردو) aur **Roman Urdu** (Urdu lafz, English haroof) do alag cheezein hain. Dono ke apne qareen aur apni mushkilat hain. Is post mein hum dono par baat karenge.

## Roman Urdu vs Urdu script — kya farq hai?

- **Roman Urdu:** Urdu lafz English haroof mein likhe jate hain — misal: "aap kaise hain". Ye WhatsApp, Facebook aur YouTube par sab se zyada istemal hoti hai. Isay parhna asaan hai aur keyboard par likhna bhi asaan hai.
- **Urdu script:** Asli Urdu haroof mein likhi jati hai — مثال: "آپ کیسے ہیں". Ye kitabon, akhbaron aur rasmi tehreeron mein istemal hoti hai. Isay likhne ke liye Urdu keyboard chahiye aur parhne wale ko Urdu parhni aani chahiye.

Aap ki audience tay karti hai ke kaunsi behtar hai. Agar aap ke readers zyada tar mobile par hain aur Roman Urdu mein baat karte hain, to Roman Urdu behtar hai — [Roman Urdu mein blog likhna](/blog/roman-urdu-blog-writer) is audience ke liye behtareen shuruaat hai. Agar aap rasmi ya adabi content likh rahe hain, to Urdu script behtar hai.

## Urdu content likhte waqt aam ghaltiyan

1. **Roman Urdu aur Urdu script milana:** Aik hi article mein dono haroof istemal karna reader ko confuse karta hai. Pehle tay karein ke kaunsi script hai, phir poora article usi mein likhein.
2. **English lafzon ka zyada istemal:** "Basically, breakfast is very important" — Urdu blog mein aise jumle ajeeb lagte hain. Jahan Urdu lafz maujood hai, wahi likhein.
3. **Conversion par andha bharosa:** Transliteration tool se convert kar ke bina parhe publish kar dena ghalti hai. Machine lafz badal deti hai, matlab nahi samajhti.
4. **RTL format bhool jana:** Urdu script mein headings aur lists ka direction theek na ho to blog parhna mushkil ho jata hai. Publish karne se pehle mobile par check karein.
5. **Lambi paragraphs:** Urdu script mein bhi chhoti paragraphs behtar hain — har paragraph 2–3 lines ka rakhein.

## Urdu content ke liye 4 tips

- **Audience pehle tay karein:** Roman Urdu ya Urdu script — ye faisla audience ke hisaab se karein, apni pasand se nahi.
- **Seedhi zabaan istemal karein:** Mushkil adabi lafz se bachein — jo baat aam bol chaal mein samajh aaye, wahi likhein.
- **Misaal dein:** "Sehatmand nashta acha hota hai" kehne se behtar hai aik khaas nashta batana — misal: "anda, paratha aur dahi".
- **Aik article, aik topic:** Har article ka aik saaf topic ho — zyada topics milayenge to reader bhatak jayega.
