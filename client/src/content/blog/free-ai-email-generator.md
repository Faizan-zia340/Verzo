## Example 1: Cold outreach email

**Situation:** Aik freelance designer aik naye client ko pehli dafa email kar raha hai.

**Prompt:** "Cold outreach email — freelance graphic designer, naye restaurant client ko, apni services offer karni hain, tone friendly-professional."

**Output (Penlix se):**

> Subject: Your restaurant's menu deserves better design
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> I came across your restaurant's Instagram last week — the food photography is great, but the menu design doesn't do it justice. I'm a freelance graphic designer, and I help restaurants turn their menus into something customers actually want to photograph.
>
> Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week? I can share two menu concepts for your restaurant, free, so you can see the difference before deciding anything.
>
> Best,
> Ahmed

Ye email kaam karegi kyun ke is mein teen cheezein hain: aik khaas observation (menu design), aik saaf offer (do free concepts), aur aik chhota ask (10-minute call). "I hope this email finds you well" jaisa khaali jumla kahin nahi.

## Achi email ke 5 usool

1. **Subject line 6–8 words:** Lambi subject line mobile par kat jati hai. "Quick follow-up — proposal from last Tuesday" theek hai; "Regarding the matter of the proposal that I sent you last week for your kind consideration" nahi.
2. **Pehla jumla reader ke bare mein:** "I came across your restaurant's Instagram" behtar hai "I am a designer with 5 years of experience" se.
3. **Aik email, aik maqsad:** Email mein do teen cheezein mangenge to jawab kisi ka nahi aayega. Aik email mein aik saaf ask ho.
4. **Mukhtasar rakhein:** Professional email aam tor par 100–150 words ki hoti hai — zyada lambi ho to call behtar hai.
5. **End par next step:** "Let me know" kamzor hai. "Would Thursday 11am work for a call?" behtar hai — jawab dena asaan hai.

## Aam ghaltiyan

- **Generic shuruaat:** "I hope you are doing well" har email mein likhna waqt zaya hai — seedhi baat se shuru karein.
- **Bohat zyada formal:** "Respected Sir, I beg to state that..." — aaj kal ki professional emails seedhi aur dostana hoti hain.
- **Bina proofread bhejna:** Aik ghalat naam ya ghalat tareekh poori email ki credibility khatam kar deti hai.
- **Sab ko aik jaisi email:** Har client ka naam, business aur masla alag hai — template aik jaisa ho sakta hai, lekin email har dafa nayi honi chahiye.
